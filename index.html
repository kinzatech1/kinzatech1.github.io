Manager By KDH

A simple tool that helps creators publish their own videos to TikTok — upload once, add a caption, and post.

What it does

  • Connect your own TikTok account securely with TikTok Login.
  • Upload a video file from your device and add your caption and hashtags.
  • Post directly to your TikTok account, or save as a draft to finish later.

Who it is for

Individual creators who make their own original videos and want a faster way to publish them to TikTok without using the TikTok app every time.

Your data

We only access what you approve during TikTok Login, and only use it to publish the videos you choose. We never sell your data. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy below.
