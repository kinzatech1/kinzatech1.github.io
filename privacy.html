Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Manager By KDH ("we", "our") is a tool that helps creators publish their own videos to TikTok. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok account information: when you connect with TikTok Login, we receive your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, as you approve on the TikTok consent screen.
  • Content you provide: the video files, captions and hashtags you choose to upload through the tool.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to operate the service (for example, upload status and error messages).

2. How we use your data

  • To publish the videos you select to your own TikTok account.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the tool.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not use your content for advertising.

3. Data sharing

We share data only with TikTok as required to perform the publishing actions you request, and as described in TikTok's own privacy policy. We do not share your data with other third parties.

4. Data retention

Uploaded video files are kept only as long as needed to complete your publishing request, after which they are deleted. You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time; on disconnect we delete the associated access tokens.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

6. Contact

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the latest version.
